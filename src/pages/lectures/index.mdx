Mondays. Each lecture sets up the paper you were asked to read and then disagrees
with some part of it, which is why the reading comes first — an argument you have
not heard the other side of is just a lecture.

Every week names its reading and says what the reading is for. Week 1 carries
slides; the rest are talked.
