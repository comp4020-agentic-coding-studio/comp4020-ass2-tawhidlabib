Twelve primary readings, one per week, plus a few that are here if the week
caught your interest. Read the primary one **before** the Monday lecture, not
after — the lecture argues with it, and an argument you have not heard the other
side of is just a lecture.

Every reading page says what the paper claims and what this course wants you to
take from it, which is not always the same thing. Several of them are studies of
places that no longer exist. That is part of the point.
