I kept a pub for eleven years. I could tell you who would be in on a Tuesday at
half four, what they drank, whose marriage was failing and who had nobody else
to tell. None of that was in any training manual. Oldenburg calls it a third
place; I called it a job, and I got good at the part of it this course is about
— noticing who is a regular, and what a room does to make someone become one.

I take the Rounds and mark Field Notes. Most of what I will teach you is how to
watch a room without changing it, and how to write down what you saw rather than
what you expected to see. Those are different skills and the second one is
harder.

**Consultation:** In the field, during Rounds, or Thursdays after the visit.
