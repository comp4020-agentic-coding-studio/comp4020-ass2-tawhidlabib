Week 9's game had a shape: zones, activities, a reason to be there. This week is
about the rooms with no activity at all — the group chat, the Discord, the
long-running forum. Somewhere to hang around, which is the whole offer.

Allen's synthesis gives us the vocabulary the older literature could not. Treat
belonging as having components — the competencies to participate, the
opportunities to do so, the motivation, and the perception that you do. Two
people in the same channel can differ on any of the four, which is why a room
that is obviously flourishing contains people getting nothing from it.

Two features of these rooms have no offline equivalent.

**There is no closing time.** Oldenburg's accessibility is about a place being
open at odd hours. A server is open at all hours, and something that never closes
never produces the shared experience of having been there late.

**The archive is total.** Every word is retained, searchable, and readable by
someone who joins in three years. A new member can read what you said before they
arrived. Nothing in the pub literature prepares us for a third place with a
perfect memory, and it changes what it costs to speak.

Thursday you observe one of these, which is harder than it sounds, because the
room does not visibly do anything and the record goes back further than your
patience.
