Pick a persistent shared space: an MMO zone, a game's social hub, a long-running
server, a regular voice channel. It has to persist between your visits and it has
to contain people who are not your friends.

**Two sittings, an hour each, at different times of day.** The composition of an
online room changes with timezone in a way a pub's does not, and one sitting will
mislead you about who the regulars are.

**Score all eight, with evidence.** Not a tick — a sentence, and the thing you
saw that justifies it. "A leveller: a level 60 spent twenty minutes helping
someone who could not repay it, and nobody remarked on it."

**Then find the one that breaks.** Every online space will have at least one
characteristic where Oldenburg's question does not quite apply, and the
interesting answer is not pass or fail but *the question is wrong here*. Say what
the right question would be.

**Cost of entry.** Money, yes, but also the hardware, the hours needed to be
competent enough to be welcome, and the fluency in how people talk there. Week 3
asked who a place keeps out. This room keeps people out with a skill floor and a
graphics card, and it does it without a door.

Bring your scoring sheet. We put several side by side, and the disagreements
about the same characteristic in different games are where the seminar goes.
