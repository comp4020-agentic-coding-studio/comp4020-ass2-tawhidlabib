Baumeister and Leary treat belonging as a standing adult motivation. Bowlby asks
where it came from, and answers that the bond between infant and caregiver is a
behavioural system with its own logic — proximity-seeking, a secure base,
distress at separation. The adult who needs somewhere to go on a Tuesday is
running on machinery built for something else.

This is here if week 2 caught you. It is long, and this course does not test it.
But if you find yourself wanting to explain *why* a regular keeps coming back
when nobody there is family, this is the literature that has thought about it
longest.
