A market, a park, a street. The three verbs in the title are three different
ways of using a public place, and only one of them is the sociable one that
Oldenburg writes about. Mingling is what he describes. Observing and lingering
are what most people are actually doing, and this paper shows they carry
benefits too.

Week 7 is involuntary places: the laundromat, the waiting room, the bus stop.
Nobody chooses them and nobody is a regular by design, and yet the same things
happen. Read this for the finding that the merely tolerated public place does
more social work than its users would ever claim for it — and then go and watch
one for an hour.
