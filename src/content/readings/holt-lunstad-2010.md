People with stronger social relationships had a 50% greater likelihood of
survival across the follow-up periods. The comparisons that made the paper
famous — that the effect is comparable to quitting smoking, and larger than
obesity or physical inactivity — are in the discussion, and they are the reason
health ministries started using the word loneliness.

Week 11 is what it costs. Read this one properly, including the limitations:
these are observational studies, the measures of "social relationship" are wildly
heterogeneous, and the causal arrow is not settled by a meta-analysis. You should
finish the week able to state the finding accurately *and* able to say what it
does not establish. Both halves get marked.
