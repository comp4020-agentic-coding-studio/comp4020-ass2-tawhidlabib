The field has used *belonging* to mean a feeling, a need, a relationship and a
place, often in the same paper. This review separates four components —
competencies, opportunities, motivations, and perceptions — and argues that most
disagreements in the literature are two people measuring different ones.

Week 12 is the last lecture and you present your proposal for a good place. Use
this framework to say precisely what your proposed place supplies: a place can
create opportunities and still fail because nobody there has the competencies,
or can be full of motivated people with nowhere to put them. Being able to name
which of the four your design targets is most of the difference between a
proposal and a wish.
