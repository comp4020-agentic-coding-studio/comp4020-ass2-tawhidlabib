Steinkuehler and Williams go through Oldenburg's characteristics one at a time
against fieldwork in *Lineage* and *Asheron's Call*, and find that the games
satisfy most of them — neutral ground, levelling, regulars, conversation as the
main activity — while producing mostly bridging rather than bonding capital.

Week 9 is the guild hall, and you do the fieldwork inside a game. This reading is
the template for that: a place gets held against the eight rules and scored.
Where it matters most is where the fit fails, so pay attention to the parts they
concede. A room you can leave by closing a laptop is not neutral ground in quite
the way a pub is.
