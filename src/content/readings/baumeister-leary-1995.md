Baumeister and Leary set themselves a high bar: to count as a fundamental
motivation, belonging has to produce effects across cognition, emotion, health
and behaviour, and it has to be satiable. They then spend thirty pages showing
it clears the bar. Two conditions matter for this course — frequent interaction,
and a stable framework of ongoing care — because a third place supplies exactly
the first and only sometimes the second.

Read it for the two conditions, and for how a review argues. The method here is
worth as much to you as the conclusion: they are building a case from other
people's data, which is very close to what you will do in the Place Audit.
