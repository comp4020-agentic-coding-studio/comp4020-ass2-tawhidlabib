description:
  The book that named the third place and gave it eight characteristics. Every

Oldenburg's claim is that a life needs three settings, not two: home, work, and
somewhere else. The somewhere else is neutral ground, it levels rank, the main
activity is conversation, it is open at odd hours, it has regulars, it is plain,
the mood is playful, and it feels like a home away from home. Those are the
eight rules, and week 1 spends the whole lecture on them.

Read it for the eight characteristics, and read it sceptically. Oldenburg was
writing in 1989, about mostly American, mostly male, mostly white rooms, and he
is frankly nostalgic in places. The question this course asks you to carry
through all twelve weeks is which of the eight are load-bearing and which are
just the 1980s. You will not be able to answer that until you have sat in a
place yourself, which is what Thursdays are for.
