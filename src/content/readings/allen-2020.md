Allen pulls together what is known about belonging across schools, workplaces,
online spaces and later life, and is useful to this course mainly for being
recent. The competencies framing — that belonging is partly a skill, partly an
opportunity, and partly a motivation — gives you vocabulary for something the
fieldwork keeps turning up: two people in the same room getting very different
amounts out of it.

Week 10 is the front porch that is a server. You will observe a Discord, which
is harder than it sounds, because there is no closing time and the archive is
total. Read the chapters on belonging online before you start, particularly on
what changes when the room keeps a perfect transcript of itself.
