Pick one place and argue about it for 2500 words.

It can be a place you have already visited for a Round, and if it is, you will
need a third visit at minimum — three occasions is the floor, not the target.

## What this is not

It is not a scorecard against Oldenburg's eight characteristics. Week 9 is the
last time that exercise is worth doing, and doing it here would be a summary of
the course rather than a use of it.

It is also not an appreciation. If your conclusion is that the place is lovely
and should be protected, you have probably skipped the part of the evidence that
makes this interesting.

## What it is

An argument about what this specific place does, for whom, at what cost, held
against what the literature would predict. The observation is the primary
material and the papers are in support — that is why observation and literature
carry equal weight and are marked separately. An audit made of citations with a
place attached will lose the first thirty and a description with no reading
behind it will lose the second.

**Disagree with something.** At least one of the readings should be wrong about
your place, or right for a reason its authors did not give. You have twelve
set papers and a room you have sat in three times; on that intersection you
are the world expert.

**Report what does not fit.** Every audit turns up an observation that spoils the
story. The Tuesday that contradicted the pattern, the regular who does not
behave like a regular, the hour when the room was somebody else's entirely. Put
it in and deal with it. Suppressing it is the single most common way this piece
goes wrong, and 25% of the mark is attached to handling it, because it is the
difference between fieldwork and advocacy.

**Say who it excludes.** Not as a paragraph of throat-clearing at the end. Week 3
onwards has been arguing that the boundary and the belonging are the same
mechanism, and an audit that cannot find the boundary has not looked.

**Ethics.** The week 7 rules apply — no photographs of people, no identifying
individuals, no observation of the excluded sites. If your place requires you to
be a customer, be one.

**Format.** PDF, 2500 words excluding references, any citation style used
consistently.
